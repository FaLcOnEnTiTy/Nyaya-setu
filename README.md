# Nyaya Setu (न्याय सेतु — "Justice Bridge")
### Undertrial Prisoner Statutory Triage Dashboard for Indian Legal Aid Authorities
*Built for HACK4SDG (Supporting UN SDG 16: Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions)*

---

## 🏛️ Executive Overview
Over **77% of India's prison population** comprises undertrial prisoners awaiting trial. Many languish in pre-trial detention beyond statutory limits simply because of poverty, inability to furnish financial sureties, or lack of legal awareness.

**Nyaya Setu** is an institutional legal-tech platform tailored for:
- **District Legal Services Authorities (DLSA)**
- **State Legal Services Authorities (SLSA)**
- **National Legal Services Authority (NALSA)**
- **Jail Welfare Officers & Prison Superintendents**
- **Panel Legal Aid Advocates & Remand Advocates**

The platform implements **Section 436A of the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), 1973** and **Section 479 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023** to proactively identify, prioritize, and draft statutory bail applications for undertrials eligible for release on personal recognizance (PR) bond.

---

## ⚖️ Non-Negotiable Safeguards

1. **Zero Automated Filing Guarantee**:
   Under the Advocates Act, 1961, court submissions require human representation. Every petition generated by Nyaya Setu is boldly watermarked: `*** DRAFT FOR ADVOCATE REVIEW BEFORE FILING — NOT AN AUTOMATED COURT SUBMISSION ***`.
2. **Strict Capital Offense Exclusion**:
   Offenses punishable by death (e.g., Section 302 IPC / Section 103 BNS) are expressly barred from automatic 436A/479 time-based release. Even if an undertrial has served 10 years, capital charges are **never** auto-cleared and are strictly routed to `MANUAL SENIOR ADVOCATE REVIEW` for regular bail under Section 439 CrPC.
3. **Bare Act Mapping Verification Safeguard**:
   Statutory sentences not yet verified against the bare acts are explicitly tagged as `Unverified Bare Act Mapping — Legal Verification Required`. Advocates can toggle verification directly from the case dossier.
4. **Multiple Offenses & Multiple Pending Cases Support**:
   - Resolves multi-offense FIRs under Section 31 CrPC / Section 25 BNSS (concurrent sentencing rule by default).
   - Activates `MULTIPLE_PENDING_CASES_FLAG` with caution notices under Section 479 BNSS Proviso 2 when the accused has pending matters across other police stations or courts.

## 🧪 Rule-Based Statutory Eligibility Engine

The engine is **100% deterministic and rule-based** (no probabilistic ML or generative hallucinations in legal qualification).

| Statutory Rule | Condition | Triage Priority | Engine Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CrPC § 436A / BNSS § 479** | Custody $\ge$ Full Maximum Sentence | `CRITICAL` | **Illegal Detention** — Mandatory immediate release / discharge petition |
| **CrPC § 436A / BNSS § 479** | Custody $\ge$ 1/2 Maximum Sentence | `HIGH` | **Eligible for Bail / PR Bond** (*Bhim Singh v. UOI 2014*) |
| **BNSS § 479(1) Proviso 1** | Custody $\ge$ 1/3 Max Sentence (First-time offender) | `HIGH` | **First-Time Offender Fast-Track Bail** (BNSS 2023) |
| **CrPC § 436 / BNSS § 478** | All offenses bailable & Custody > 7 days | `HIGH` | **Indigent Prisoner Deemed** — Entitled to bail on self-bond without sureties |
| **CrPC § 436A Proviso** | Offense punishable with Death | `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` | **Statutory Exclusion** — Merits review under CrPC § 439 |
| **BNSS § 479 Proviso 2** | Multiple pending inquiries or trials | `CAUTION` | Multi-court disclosure required |

## 🚢 Quickstart & Deployment

### 1. Install & Setup
```bash
npm install
npx prisma generate
npx prisma db push
npx ts-node prisma/seed.ts
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)

### 2. Deploy to Vercel + PostgreSQL (Neon / Supabase)
1. In `prisma/schema.prisma`, update the datasource provider:
   ```prisma
   datasource db {
     provider = "postgresql"
     url      = env("DATABASE_URL")
   }
   ```
2. Set `DATABASE_URL` in your Vercel Environment Variables.
3. Deploy to Vercel:
   ```bash
   vercel
   ```
